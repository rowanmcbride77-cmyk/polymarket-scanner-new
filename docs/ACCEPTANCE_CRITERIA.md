# Acceptance criteria
## 30-day Top 100
- Candidate pool is Polymarket's overall monthly leaderboard.
- Measure settlement outcomes over the trailing 30 days.
- Require at least 90% win rate and at least 10 genuinely closed markets.
- Count only positions verifiably held through resolution.
- Exclude early exits from both wins and losses.
- Exclude ambiguous same-second entries/exits and unverifiable settlement records.
- Rank qualifying wallets by Polymarket monthly leaderboard P&L.
- Publish at most 100 unique wallets, with ranks 1 through N.
- No partial audit or unverified data may replace the last validated generation.
## Publication
Tests pass; sampled wallets reconcile to source records; the full candidate audit completes; and the website displays the exact validated 30-day leaderboard generation.
